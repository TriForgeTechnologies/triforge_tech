Our Origin

Why We Started TriForge

We saw businesses struggling with the same fundamental problem: technology partners who were either technically brilliant but strategically blind, or visionary but unable to execute. The market had vendors. It lacked partners.

TriForge Technologies was founded to fill that gap. Three co-founders with distinct but complementary expertise—engineering, design, and product strategy—came together with the belief that the best technology solutions emerge when these disciplines are deeply integrated.

The name says it all: three forces forged into one.

FAQ

Common Questions

Straight answers to the questions clients ask us most. No corporate jargon.

How do you handle projects when TriForge is a new company?

+
Being newly founded doesn't mean inexperienced. Our co-founders bring years of professional engineering, design, and delivery experience from prior companies and freelance work. We apply rigorous processes—discovery workshops, formal requirements, sprint planning, code reviews, and structured QA—and we put our full attention on every engagement because each client relationship is foundational to our growth.

What's your typical project timeline and how do you estimate cost?

+
Timelines vary by scope. A focused MVP typically runs 8–12 weeks. A full enterprise application might be 4–9 months. We provide detailed project estimates after a discovery session where we understand your requirements, constraints, and success criteria. Pricing is transparent and milestone-based—you never receive an invoice that surprises you.

Do you work with international clients?

+
Absolutely. We work with clients in the UK, UAE, and beyond. Our team is comfortable with remote-first collaboration using tools like Slack, Notion, Figma, and GitHub. We're flexible on time zones and maintain clear async communication norms so international clients always have visibility into project progress.

Who owns the code and IP after the project?

+
You do. Upon final payment, all code, design assets, documentation, and related IP transfer fully to the client. We don't use proprietary frameworks or locked-in tooling. Our goal is for you to be able to take what we build and run with it—with or without us.

What does your post-launch support look like?

+
We offer tiered maintenance packages covering bug fixes, security patching, infrastructure monitoring, and feature iterations. Standard post-launch support includes a 30-day warranty period for any defects. Beyond that, clients can choose monthly retainer arrangements from light-touch monitoring to fully managed development bandwidth.

How do you handle confidentiality and data security?

+
We sign NDAs before any substantive project discussions. Internally, we follow strict data handling protocols: no client data stored on personal devices, encrypted communications, and access control policies that follow the principle of least privilege. For regulated industries like healthcare, we explicitly architect systems for compliance from the ground up.

What makes TriForge different from a typical outsourcing firm?

+
Three key differences: (1) You get a founding team personally invested in the outcome. (2) Our architecture decisions are made for your long-term scalability, not fast billing. (3) We think like product owners—we'll push back on requirements that don't serve users and proactively suggest better approaches.
